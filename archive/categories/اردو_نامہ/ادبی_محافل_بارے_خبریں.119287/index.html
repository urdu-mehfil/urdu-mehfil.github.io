SAQAFATI MELA - 2023 - Islamabad
ثقافتی میلہ 2023
When: 16th June till 20th June, 2023
Where: National Institute of Folk and Traditional Heritage (Lok Virsa), Garden Avenue, Shakarparian, Islamabad
National Institute of Folk and Traditional Heritage (Lok Virsa), National Heritage and Culture Division, Government of Pakistan is holding a five-day “Saqafati Mela” from Friday, 16th June to Tuesday, 20th June, 2023 at its Complex located at Garden Avenue, Shakarparian.
The festival aims at promoting Pakistan’s folk heritage, strengthening national harmony and integration among all federating units through a cultural perspective and contributing in minimizing poverty among master artisans by providing them a platform to demonstrate their skills at national level.
The main features would include:-
Special Events:
• Inaugural Ceremony on Friday, 16th June, 2023 at 7 pm at Lok Virsa open air theatre
• Cultural Diversity Show featuring folk artists and folk musicians on Saturday, 17th June, 2023 at 8 pm at Lok Virsa open air theatre
• Performances by PNCA Performing Art Theatre on Sunday, 18th June, 2023 at 8 pm at Lok Virsa open air theatre
• Musical Fusion Night (for invitees only) on Monday, 19th June, 2023 at 8 pm at Lok Virsa Rooftop Theatre
Daily Attractions:
• Exhibition of Artisans-at-work
• Exhibition of Folk Paintings
• Folk Dance Performances
• Performances by Traditional Drummers (Dholis)
• Craft Bazaar
• Folk Cuisine
• Puppet Show
• Children Play Area
Mela timings:
12 noon to 11 pm daily
Mela Entry Ticket
Rs. 100 per head
Rs. 50 per head (discounted) for students

The English Literary Society, Lahore, Pakistan in association with The Progressive Writers' Association, Pakistan will organise a session on "The Black Humour"also known as "The Dark Comedy" or the "Black Comedy "0n August 31,2024 at Pak Tea House at 6:00P.M
Black Humour is a style of writing comedy about serious or prohibited matters or attitudes.It also reflects absurdity, senselessness and irrationality.Many popular creative writers have expressed in this style of comedy like Jonathan Swift, Joseph Heller,Kurt Vonnegut,John Barth, Philip Roth, John Irving,Martin Amis and other bys.Much of their writings are a part of Postmodernism literature.This comedy started in 1960s in America. The impact of World War 2 is also its theme. Its founder is Andre Breton who also founded" Surrealism "the literary movement about process of thought.The Dark Comedy is not a new style of writing comedy.It can be also traced in Century 18 in the novel "Gulliver's Travels"by Jonathan Swift.
The attendees will express their views on the occasion.
